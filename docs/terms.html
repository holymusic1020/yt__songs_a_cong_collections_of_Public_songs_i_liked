Terms of Service

  1. Service. The Nix Speech TikTok integration lets its owner upload and schedule short original music videos to the owner's own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API.
  2. Ownership. All media posted is original work owned by this account's operator, and is its sole responsibility.
  3. Access. The integration acts only with OAuth consent granted by the owner, and stops acting the moment that consent is revoked.
  4. Prohibited use. No spam, no scraping, and no posting on behalf of other users. The service is used by the owner, on the owner's account only.
  5. Availability. The service is automated and provided on a best-effort basis; schedules may slip due to quota or platform-side limits without notice.
  6. Liability. The legality of posted content is the operator's own matter; the automation provides the pipeline as-is.
  7. Revocation. Disconnect at any time from TikTok (Settings → Privacy → Apps). All stored tokens then become useless and are removed.

Scope of the TikTok integration

This app requests exactly three permissions:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which account is being connected
  • video.upload — to deliver a video to that account
  • video.publish — to publish that delivered video

Only one TikTok account is ever connected: the artist's own.
