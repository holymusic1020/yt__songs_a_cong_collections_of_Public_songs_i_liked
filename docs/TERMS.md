# Terms of Service — Nix Speech (TikTok app)

_Effective: 2026-09-03_

1. **Service.** The Nix Speech TikTok integration lets its owner upload and
   schedule short original music videos to the owner's own TikTok account
   using the TikTok Content Posting API.
2. **Ownership.** All media posted is original work owned by this account's
   operator and is its sole responsibility.
3. **Access.** The integration acts only with OAuth consent granted by the
   owner and stops acting when consent is revoked.
4. **Prohibited use.** No spam, no scraping, no posting on behalf of other
   users. The service is used by the owner on the owner's account only.
5. **Availability.** The service is automated ("best effort"); schedules may
   slip on quota or platform-side limits without notice.
6. **Liability.** Posted content's legality is the operator's own matter; the
   automation provides the pipeline as-is.
7. **Revocation.** Disconnect from TikTok (Settings → Privacy → Apps) at any
   time; all stored tokens are then useless and removed.
